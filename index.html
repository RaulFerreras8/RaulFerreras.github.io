★ Case study 1 · Solar & BESS site screening

Automated 4D Solar Yield & Shading Site-Screening Engine

The Business Problem

Manual terrain, slope, and hourly shadow analysis across candidate parcels slows down solar/BESS site selection and ties up senior analysts in desktop GIS software.

The Engineering Solution

Built a containerized PostGIS + FastAPI + pvlib pipeline ingesting 1m USGS DEM and building footprints to run automated hourly 4D shadow ray-tracing and irradiance modeling at scale.

Measurable Impact

Processes 2,080 buildings (≈452 ha) in ~10 minutes end-to-end without manual intervention, outputting queryable kWh/m² yield losses via REST API directly into QGIS or web dashboards.

Energy Vulnerability Index TurinItaly · QGIS · Census tracts
Problem

Identify which census tracts in Turin concentrate the highest energy vulnerability, combining socioeconomic, housing and demographic indicators to support targeted policy intervention.

Methodology

Construction of a composite Energy Vulnerability Index (EVI) using normalized indicators from ISTAT census data at the finest available spatial resolution. Spatial autocorrelation analysis with Getis-Ord Gi* hotspot detection and Local Indicators of Spatial Association (LISA) to identify statistically significant vulnerability clusters.

Result

High-resolution vulnerability maps and cluster analysis revealing spatial patterns invisible in aggregate statistics, enabling decision-makers to prioritize intervention areas with precision.

Urban Heat Island Analysis PhiladelphiaUSA · Landsat 8 · QGIS
Problem

Identify where Philadelphia's urban heat concentrates at the neighborhood scale and quantify the temperature gap between the hottest built-up districts and the city's green and riparian corridors, providing spatial evidence for heat-equity and urban greening policy.

Methodology

Land Surface Temperature (LST) retrieval from Landsat 8 Band 10 (TIRS) using the radiometric calibration and emissivity correction workflow (USGS Collection 2, Level-2). Thermal classification into nine LST ranges (≤18 °C to >46 °C) overlaid with OpenDataPhilly neighborhood boundaries.

Result

High-resolution LST map revealing a pronounced heat island in central and south Philadelphia (38–46+ °C) against cooler green and waterfront buffers (18–26 °C), with intra-city differentials above 28 °C.

Urban Service Accessibility Index BarcelonaSpain · QGIS · OpenStreetMap
Problem

Measure the real accessibility of urban services across Barcelona's census sections to identify underserved areas and support urban planning decisions.

Methodology

Construction of a composite Urban Service Accessibility Index integrating OpenStreetMap data and Barcelona's open data portal at census section level. Multi-criteria spatial analysis combining proximity, density and coverage of key urban services.

Result

Accessibility classification map identifying high, medium-high, medium-low and low accessibility zones, providing a spatial evidence base for equitable urban service planning.
